# Plan: Dominion (round-based mode, 2v2 lane map + 3v3v3 on today's arena) (2026-10-02)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development, under workflow v1.4
> (`C:\UniStuff\Y3\MinorSkilled\Resources\core\`): one brief per task in `Resources/loops/Limit Test/briefs/`, one sonnet
> implementer under the Editor lock, one read-only **opus** review per task (shared match rules = networking), fixes
> re-reviewed, the controller's own check, logged, one commit per task, push after the review's fixes land. Starts after
> the lobby plan (`2026-10-02-lobby.md`) has landed.

**Goal (play terms):** a second mode in the lobby's create screen: best-of-three timed rounds where held zones score
points, a free build per round with weapon tiers opening round by round, a blue respawn shield, the 3v3v3 centre paying
200 points every 30 s, and sudden death in a shrinking circle; on today's arena (3v3v3) and on a new one-lane map (2v2).

**Architecture:** `DominionDirector` beside `MatchDirector`, chosen by the room's `GameModeDefinition.family`; round state
in Room Properties (`dRnd dStg dEnd dPts dWins dCtr dSd dWin`), shield in Player Properties (`dShd dBlk`); pure rules in
`Assets/scripts/Dominion/Rules/`; existing resets, shop, territory and fog reused. A second scene for the 2v2 map, loaded
by Photon's scene sync. No RPC added/renamed/removed.

**Tech stack:** Unity 6000.0.70f1, URP, Photon PUN 2, TextMeshPro, NUnit edit-mode tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-dominion-design.md`. **Mockups + picks:**
https://claude.ai/artifact/8zDa3iexpnsFYnUH8cZ5Pi. **Every answer and the info-card texts:**
`Resources/loops/Limit Test/lobby-dominion-design-notes.md`.

## Tasks (in order; each 30-60 min + review)
Order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 are the core on today's arena (rounds run, zones score, capitals behave). Then 5 (shop), 6
(respawn + heal), 7 (shield), 8 (sudden death), 9 (HUD and screens), 10 → 11 (the 2v2 scene), 12 (2v2 end to end), 13
(cards, modes on, logs), 14 (the whole check), 15 (GDD). 2 waits for 1's review; 11 waits for 10's review.

### Task 1: Dominion rules + DominionConfig (D1-D9)
- `DominionConfig` (menu `OverPower/Dominion Config`, plain tooltips, every field of the spec's Design values; defaults
  from the decisions and `assumptions-for-tudor.md`).
- `DominionRules`: `Stage NextStage(stage, round, wins, ...)`, `RoundWinner(int[] points)` (−1 on a tie for first),
  `MatchWinner(int[] wins, roundsToWin)`, `SuddenDeathTeams(int[] wins, teamsInMatch)` (the tied leaders),
  `PointsThisTick(owners, isSpawnZone, isCentre, perTier)`, `NextCentrePayoutMs(roundStartMs, nowMs, interval)`,
  `CentreHolderAt(...)`, `BountyDue(holdSinceMs, nowMs, holdSeconds)`. `RespawnShieldRules`: `IsUp(endMs, nowMs)`,
  `EndsOnDamageDealt`, `CountsForCapture(shieldUp) = !shieldUp`. `SuddenDeathRules`: `Radius(startMs, nowMs, shrinkSec,
  startRadius, finalRadius)`, `IsOutside(pos, centre, radius)`. `DominionShopRules`: `MaxWeaponDepth(round)`,
  `ArmorUpgradesAllowed(round)`.
- **Tests first:** a round with equal top points has no winner; the first to the target wins the match; 1-1 after round 3
  (with a tied round) → sudden death; 3v3v3 1-1-1 → all three in sudden death, 1-1-0 → the two; spawns add nothing; the
  centre adds nothing per tick and pays only at its times; a bounty is due only after an unbroken hold; the shield blocks
  until its time and ends on damage dealt; the radius never grows and stops at the final radius; round 1 allows depth 0
  and no armor, round 2 depth 1 + one, round 3 depth 2 + two. No numbers pinned (relations only). **Review:** opus.

### Task 2: The round flow in the room (D1, D3, A1) - 2-3 clients, Dominion 3v3v3 on today's arena
- `DominionDirector` (active when the room's mode family is Dominion): on go-live writes round 1 (`dRnd`, `dStg` = round,
  `dEnd`); at `dEnd` the master scores the round (`dWins`), writes the break or sudden death or `dWin`; on every round edge
  each client runs the per-round reset (`PlayerLifecycle.ResetForMatchStart`, master `BuildingManager.ResetForMatchStart`
  all neutral). Conquest's knockout recompute stays off in Dominion; the map shrink never runs. Master migration carries on
  from the room values.
- **Check:** short rounds/breaks in memory; three rounds play through on 3 clients (recorder: stage, round, end time per
  client, resets seen); the master quits mid-round and the round still ends on time. **Review:** opus.

### Task 3: Points, bounties, the centre payout (D2, D8, D9) - 2-3 clients
- Master: once per second `dPts += PointsThisTick` (capitals and spawns excluded); bounty paid on a capture after an
  unbroken hold; centre lump at `dCtr` to the holder at that moment, next `dCtr` written; `CentreScan` off in Dominion.
- **Check:** one team holds a zone → its points rise at the configured rate on every client; centre payout lands once to
  the holder captured 1 s before the payout; a bounty pays on a capture after the hold. **Review:** opus.

### Task 4: Territory in Dominion (D12) - 2 clients
- Capitals not capturable in Dominion (`Building capture.cs` refuses, ring hidden), still count as held for adjacency;
  no gold (`GoldWallet` silent); health packs and OverPower unchanged.
- **Check:** standing in an enemy capital does nothing; capturing next to your capital works; no gold ticks. **Review:**
  opus.

### Task 5: The build each round (D4) - 2 clients
- The shop in Dominion: free, open only in the break (and once for a mid-round joiner before spawning), weapon depth and
  armor upgrades limited by `DominionShopRules`, greyed tiers labelled "Round 2" / "Round 3".
- **Check:** round 1 refuses a family; the break before round 2 allows one family + one armor upgrade; the shop closes when
  the round starts; picks reset at the next round. Captures of the greyed tiers. **Review:** opus.

### Task 6: Respawn time and spawn healing (D5) - 2 clients
- Dominion respawn = the size's fixed time; spawn healing out of combat at the capital rate and in combat at `Spawn Heal
  In Combat`: the 3v3v3 capital circle; the 2v2 healing area (Task 11 places its volume; the component reads a box or a
  circle).
- **Check:** three deaths in a row each respawn after the same time (recorder); health rises in combat at the lower rate
  inside the area and not outside. **Review:** opus.

### Task 7: The respawn shield (D6, A2) - 2-3 clients
- `RespawnShield` on the player prefab: on respawn the owner writes `dShd`; `PlayerHealth` blocks damage while it is up
  (victim-side) and writes `dBlk`; everyone draws the bubble (scale from the Invulnerability ring × `Shield Bubble Scale`)
  and pops "BLOCKED" on a new `dBlk`; the owner clears `dShd` when `RPC_DamageCredit` reports damage it dealt or when time
  is up; zone presence skips shielded players.
- **Check:** a shielded player takes no damage from a second client's shots (recorder on the victim), "BLOCKED" shows on
  the shooter's screen (capture), the shield ends the moment the shielded player hits someone, a shielded player in a zone
  neither captures nor blocks. **Review:** opus.

### Task 8: Sudden death (D7, A4) - 2-3 clients
- `dSd` written by the master when sudden death starts; everyone reset alive at their spawn, no shield, no respawns
  (deaths wait), points frozen; `SuddenDeathZone` draws the ring and the red outside (world + minimap) from
  `SuddenDeathRules.Radius`; each client damages its own player outside; the last team with a living player → `dWin`.
- **Check:** forced sudden death on 2 clients; the radius per second matches the rule on both (recorder); a player outside
  loses health at the configured rate; the last survivor's team wins. **Review:** opus.

## Risks
- Scene sync (Task 10) touches every system in the scene: the prefab move must be behaviour-neutral (regression check).
- Points written every second by the master: a master switch can skip one tick (accepted; the next master resumes).
- The shield ending relies on the victim's damage credit reaching the attacker; a lost message keeps the shield to its
  time limit (accepted).

## Out of scope
Rematch, kill feed, new sounds, round scoreboard tab, AI players, Conquest changes.
